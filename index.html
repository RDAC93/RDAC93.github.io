Proposed research directions

Three questions for a future fellowship

Potential projects to shape together with the host.

01 / Vascular health

Early vascular dysfunction

Can detailed metabolic and lipoprotein profiles identify molecular patterns associated with endothelial dysfunction and subclinical atherosclerosis?

Potential approachLink circulating biomarkers to human vascular imaging or endothelial function measures and clinical phenotypes.

02 / Inflammation

Inflammatory and metabolic risk

How do chronic low-grade inflammation and altered lipoprotein composition interact in cardiometabolic health?

Potential approachIntegrate inflammatory biomarkers with metabolic profiling in human cohorts, accounting for major confounders.

03 / Prevention

Metabolic ageing and risk stratification

Do deviations from expected age- and sex-specific metabolic profiles add information about future cardiovascular outcomes?

Potential approachUse longitudinal cohorts, time-to-event models and external validation where available.

These are proposed research questions, not completed or funded projects. A final proposal would depend on host expertise, available data, ethical approvals and fellowship requirements.

01 / Research

Research at the intersection
of metabolism and health.

My work connects molecular phenotyping with human health, with a particular interest in the early metabolic changes associated with cardiovascular disease and endothelial dysfunction.

BACKGROUND

I completed my PhD in Molecular Biology and Biomedicine at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) in October 2026, awarded Cum Laude and the International Doctorate distinction. My doctoral research at CIC bioGUNE focused on age- and sex-specific lipoprotein profiles in large international cohorts.

APPROACH

My background spans clinical metabolomics, lipoprotein phenotyping and biomarker discovery. I work across the analytical and computational workflow, from biofluid sample preparation and NMR acquisition to statistical modelling and clinical interpretation.
